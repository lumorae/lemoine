## Body

**You weren't made to blend in. So why does your brand?**

Here's an uncomfortable test. Pull up your homepage. Cover the logo. Now ask: if I
swapped it with a competitor's, would anyone notice?

For most founders, the honest answer is *no.* Same stock-photo calm. Same safe
palette. Same three words everyone uses — "elevate," "empower," "innovate." It's
not bad. That's exactly the problem. **Bad gets ignored. Forgettable gets
*replaced.***

---

**The one idea: differentiation is a decision, not a decoration.**

A standout brand isn't the result of a prettier logo or a trendier font. It's the
result of one decision made early and held with nerve: *what will we be known for
that our competitors can't, or won't, claim?*

Everything downstream — color, type, voice, the way a package opens in someone's
hands — is just that decision, made visible. Skip the decision and no amount of
craft saves you. Make it, and even simple design starts to feel inevitable.

So before the next rebrand, redesign, or "let's freshen things up," answer three
questions:

1. **What's the one thing we'd defend?** Not five values. One.
2. **What would competitors never say about themselves?** Go there.
3. **If we removed our name, would the work still be unmistakably us?** If not, the
   identity is borrowed, not built.

---

**Proof:** Every brand we build at Lemoine starts here — strategy first, beauty
second — which is exactly why the beauty *lands*. Distinctive isn't loud. It's
specific.
