## Layout (maps to Flodesk's visual blocks)

1. **Hook** — 1–2 lines. A sharp observation or a contrarian truth. Earn the scroll.

2. **The one idea** — the single takeaway this issue delivers. Teach something a
   founder could use tomorrow. Use a concrete example or before/after.

3. **The proof / mini case** — show, don't claim. A line about real work or a
   visual. This is where the Lemoine craft does the persuading.

4. **The single CTA** — *one* button. Match it to the goal:
   - Growth months → "Forward this to a founder who needs it" + a soft "not subscribed? join here."
   - Sales months → "Let's build what matters →" (book a call).

5. **Sign-off** — Johnny, human and brief.

6. **Footer** — web-version link, social links, unsubscribe.

---

## Pre-send checklist
- [ ] One idea only — cut anything that competes with it
- [ ] One primary CTA
- [ ] Subject ≠ preview text; both earn the open
- [ ] Mobile read-through (single column, big tap targets)
- [ ] Forward/share line present (growth months)
- [ ] Web version on for social repurposing
- [ ] Links work; UTM tags on CTA
- [ ] Sending to the right segment(s)
