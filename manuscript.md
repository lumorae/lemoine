## Entry 01 — Lake Siskiyou (continuous prose, photos IMG_8285 → IMG_8286 → IMG_8287)

*(note across the top margin): The part of us that believes it's running the show experiences surrender at catastrophe.*

I kayaked across Lake Siskiyou today. Something I've done 3 or 4 times on my journey from San Diego to Ashland — seeing friends, biking, camping along the way. Today is extra peaceful, and just a week before my 34th birthday and 5 days before 5 ceremonies of Ayahuasca. I'll take all the peace I can get. I think it's just before 5pm — during a heatwave that's unbearable at the quite noisy campground, but perfect for kayaking and swimming. It's the second time in a row that I forgot my paddle. Last year I picked one up at REI for $300. It shocked me how expensive they are. I was excited to use it this year — it's lightweight and carbon fiber. But this rental one does just the trick. It seems like I forgot a few things for this journey, which is an indication of how busy and honestly distracted I've been since Marisa and I traveled across Portugal, Italy + Sicily. This spot is beautiful right now. Soft wind gusts and gentle lake waves wash up onto a rocky, branch-ridden shore — covered by big trees — so the dappled shading made it attractive to me. I got here, pulled up my kayak the best I could, and saw a large, perfect stump 20 yards away. I carried it over, somehow not eating it[?], and grounded it into the water. It's the perfect place to meditate, with the sun on my face and the water swishing consistently, as leaves dance and sing in harmony above me. Took a little dip as well. Lake's so slippery[?], my friend. Now I'm just reflecting on my day a bit and will get back to meditating before I ship myself off across the lake. Grateful I have a paddle at all. — Ahh — gratitude… what a shift of energy you can create.

Why do I keep doing this work? It doesn't get any easier. And it's not like it's cheap either. There is an innate push, deep within my "Urpa Sonkoy"[?] — or the deepest part of my heart. I just keep going. I keep meditating. I keep learning sound-healing instruments, buying and learning. Like a calling from God. I keep traveling and sitting w/ Aya. I keep going through the darkest of places to visit my shadow parts, to undo old patterns that don't need to be part of me anymore. I want to do it for Marisa and the sake of our relationship. I don't want to hurt her + I don't want her to share my dark patterns w/ her — I don't want her to feel unsafe. Oftentimes in a tough argument, I'll get absolute + extreme + threaten to leave. It's terrible. I recognize it as me obtaining[?] power + I suppose. As hard as it is to write: control. If I say this in a heated argument — it makes her realize how serious I am. I need to leave this behind me and work on it [through] these ceremonies. How to let go, take the high road each time, and surrender. I can say what I want and do it with compassion + Urpa Sonkoy[?]. These ceremonies, I'll be focusing on surrender. Surrender the ego, and surrender to the medicine. Let go of the ego. Leave it behind. I don't need to be right. And when Mama Aya shows me something, perhaps it is death… I will be ready to accept and follow the sacred path, instead of grasping[?] anything to hold on to reality. Instead, she's showing me a future reality, and the gift of dissolution + leaving this place, this body, this world. When I can surrender + accept this beauty, this notion of mortality, and face the fear + follow through… then I know something incredible is on the other side. And I can allow[?] the ego to survive, and it's part of me, but I can also not let it run the show. You can be my greatest friend, even. But when you're dissolving during Aya ceremonies, just know we'll be okay. We can connect after. It may feel like an actual death, and

maybe it is — who knows. But what's on the other side is a better, easier, more connected life. It'll be hard, even excruciating at times, but it's just the medicine helping us get to where we need to go. I deserve it. My relationship deserves it. My career deserves [it]. Everyone in my life will benefit from what's about to come. And when we get through it, I can't wait to share my Urpa Sonkoy[?] with the world. I need to make Marisa feel safe, even when things are damn hard. Especially then. Stay calm. Be compassionate + curious. Compassion is the dissolver of obstacles.
