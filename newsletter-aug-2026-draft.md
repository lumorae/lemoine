I've spent the summer redesigning the Lemoine site. Again. On purpose.

Somewhere between the sketches and the code, I realized I wasn't really redesigning a website. I was redesigning how much of myself I let you see.

For years this brand has been the studio owner and the designer. Clean lines, client work, polish. But you and I both know there's more under the hood — the guy who paints, who skates, who hums melodies into his phone at red lights. So I'm rebuilding johnnylemoine.com to hold all of it: an ever-evolving platform for the work, the art, and the human making both.

It will never be finished. That's the point.

(About the humming: this summer I'm weaving my own music into the site, with songs and videos headed for Spotify, SoundCloud, and YouTube. That story gets its own email next month. I'm equal parts thrilled and terrified — which is how I know it's right.)

- **Music = one parenthetical, zero links.** It's the September email's headline moment.
